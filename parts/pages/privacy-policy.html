Privacy Policy

How Black Sets collects, handles, and protects your information.

1. Information We Collect

Black Sets collects personal information that you provide directly to us when contacting us through our website, submitting an enquiry form, or subscribing to production announcements. This information may include your name, email address, telephone number, company, and project details.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information solely to respond to your production enquiries, manage our client and supplier relationships, provide quotations and technical set design services, and comply with legal obligations.

3. Data Security

We implement robust technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

4. Cookies

Our website uses standard essential session cookies to ensure the website functions securely and efficiently. We do not use intrusive tracking cookies.

5. Your Rights

Under applicable data protection legislation (including the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018), you have the right to access, rectify, or request deletion of your personal data. To exercise these rights, please contact us at info@blacklab.com or blackvfx@blackhangarstudios.com.
