At Black Sets, we pride ourselves on creating immersive, visually striking environments for film and TV productions of any scale. Our craftsmen, designers and fabricators work with precision from the first sketches to the final installation. Every set is engineered to support storytelling, enhance cinematography and meet the practical needs of production crews.

We manage concept development, technical design, material planning, fabrication and on-site build with a focus on accuracy, safety and efficiency. Our workshop delivers consistent results that align with your schedule and budget.

Whether you need a full-scale world, a detailed hero piece or modular elements that can adapt across multiple stages, we provide solutions that help your production run smoothly and look exceptional on camera.

Based at Black Hangar Studios with access to large stage facilities and state-of-the-art workshop technology, our team delivers complete physical builds, 3D laser scans, and virtual production assets.
