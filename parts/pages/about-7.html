Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to the most common questions about our services, processes, and policies. If you don’t see what you’re looking for, feel free to reach out – our team is always here to help.

What types of sets do Black Sets build?+

We build everything from bespoke hero interior and exterior environments to modular soundstage sets, period locations, futuristic sci-fi structures, and practical practical rigs for feature films, TV dramas, commercials, and music videos.

Where are your workshops and soundstages located?+

We are based at Black Hangar Studios on Lasham Airfield, Alton, Hampshire (GU34 5SR), offering direct access to large soundstages, outdoor tank facilities, aircraft backlot sets, and comprehensive carpentry workshops.

What are Virtual Sets and 3D Scans?+

We perform high-resolution 3D laser scanning and photogrammetry of our physical sets and locations. This produces millimeter-accurate 3D digital environments for VFX matchmoving, virtual production LED volumes (Unreal Engine), and pre-visualisation.

Can you handle international productions or on-location builds?+

Yes. In addition to our UK studio facilities, we regularly fabricate modular components for shipment and assemble camera-ready sets on locations across the UK, Europe, North America, and internationally.

How early in pre-production should we engage Black Sets?+

We recommend contacting us as early as concept development. Early involvement allows our technical designers to offer cost-effective structural solutions, material planning, and 3D scan integration.
